# Known limitations after Milestone D

- The rigged Soldier model and its animation clips load at runtime from the Three.js example asset host; gameplay requires network access to that asset URL. A procedural fallback character remains available if the download fails or times out.
- The model source provides idle, walk, and run clips. The controller corrects the model's forward-axis mismatch, normalizes imported height, aligns the lowest mesh point to the ground plane, adjusts gait playback rate, and adds a soft procedural contact shadow. If no dedicated jump clip is present, it synthesizes a simple jump pose from rig bones; this is less polished than a purpose-authored jump/fall/land set.
- Player physics use a lightweight custom controller with gravity, jump impulse, horizontal acceleration, axis-separated AABB/circle collision, and fixed ground height. The car uses custom fixed-step rigid-body-style longitudinal dynamics and yaw steering with a conservative AABB impact response, not a general-purpose rigid-body engine. Vehicle suspension is a visual spring/probe approximation on the flat test terrain; there is no tire friction model, drivetrain simulation, deformable body, damage, or dynamic terrain.
- Main building masses, diner walls and furniture, selected street furniture, curbs/rails, and the vehicle use simplified static collision boxes. Palm foliage, facade details, the ocean, and some decorative geometry are not solid obstacles. The diner is the only enterable building; its roof is intentionally open/omitted so the interior remains visible from the gameplay camera.
- Camera obstruction uses raycasts against marked scene objects without a spatial index; this is suitable for the small test district but should be optimized as the world grows.
- The district is still a compact procedural prototype, not a full city. The Coastline Diner is the only enterable interior and has one simple coffee interaction. Only one player-drivable coupe is implemented; there are no NPCs, civilian traffic, missions, map routing, save system, or police/wanted system.
- Manual Chrome gameplay verification of vehicle entry/exit, city collision, diner access/interaction, and walking transitions, plus target-hardware FPS testing, remain pending.
